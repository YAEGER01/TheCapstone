President Help Guide

Role-specific help for the President dashboard

Your Role: As president, you lead the organization - submitting the monthly deduction request and granting final approval to all verified entries.

This guide walks you through these tasks step by step:

  • Monthly Dues Process: submit the monthly deduction request.
  • Final Approvals: grant final approval to monthly deductions, aid requests, and generated reports.
Note: Submit the deduction request; it then moves to the Treasurer for recording.

SUBMIT THE DEDUCTION REQUEST

1.
Access the Finance Section: Navigate to the Finance tab on the main navigation menu.
2.
Open Monthly Deductions: Select Monthly Deduction from the sub-navigation options.
3.
Specify the Period: Choose the target Month from the dropdown menu.
4.
Add New Entry: Click the Add Item button to open the deduction details form.
5.
Enter Deduction Details: Select the appropriate Purpose category (Monthly Due, Aid, Other, etc.) and enter the total Amount.
6.
Attach Required Documents: Upload the signed versions of both the Request Letter and the Deduction Sheet.
7.
Submit for Processing: Review the entered information and click Submit.
Note: Review the Auditor’s verified entries and grant final approval.
1.
Approve Monthly Deductions: Navigation: Finance > Monthly Deductions
  • View monthly dues pending approval.
  • Review the verified details and grant approval.
2.
Approve Aid Requests: Navigation: Final Approval
  • View aid requests pending approval.
  • Review the request details and grant approval.
3.
Approve Generated Reports: Navigation: Reports > Fund Reports Approval
  • View pending generated reports.
  • Review the report details and grant final approval.
