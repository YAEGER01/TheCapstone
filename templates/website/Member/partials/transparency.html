{% comment %} Member Dashboard — Transparency tab. Three panels, each with a deliberately different audience: 1. Financial Overview — organisation-wide and always unmasked. This is the association's own position, which a member is entitled to read. 2. Cash Flow — the member's OWN money in / money out. 3. My Records — the member's OWN dues, contributions and payments. Collections has its own panel no longer: the member's monthly obligation was already reported twice over, once as a per-month table and again as movements in Cash Flow, and the two could be made to disagree. Cash Flow plus the Monthly Dues matrix in My Records say the same thing once. There is no disclosure bar, no masking and no reveal step: every figure on this tab is shown as recorded. Nothing is rendered server-side, so no financial value ever lands in the page context. {% endcomment %}
