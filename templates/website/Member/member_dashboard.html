Data Privacy Notice

The ISUCauFA Finance Management System collects membership, contribution, and claims information — including contact details, employment status, and supporting documents — to process dues, benefits, and financial reports for CAUFA officers, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Your data is used only for CAUFA membership and finance administration and is not shared with outside parties without your consent, except when required by law or by the association's approval process (Secretary → Treasurer → Auditor → President).

Records are kept for as long as you remain a CAUFA member and for a defined period afterward for audit purposes. You may request to view, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting the CAUFA Secretary.

  1. Acceptance. Using this system means you accept these Terms and the Data Privacy Notice above.
  2. Who can access this system. Limited to the President, Treasurer, Auditor, and Secretary. Members get read-only access to their own ledger only.
  3. Your account. Your temporary password is for one-time login only. You must set a new password before continuing, and must not share your password or invite link with anyone.
  4. Data we collect and why. Membership fee and dues records, medical/death aid claim documents, and beneficiary details — collected solely to process claims and produce reports required by the CAUFA Constitution and By-Laws.
  5. Data retention. Records are kept for the duration of membership and a defined period afterward for audit purposes. (Retention length is a recommendation — confirm the exact figure with the Treasurer or adviser.)
  6. Accuracy of submissions. You are responsible for the accuracy of documents you submit; false or altered documents may be grounds for claim denial.
  7. System monitoring. Login attempts, approvals, and claim decisions are logged for audit purposes.
  8. No guarantee of uninterrupted access. The officially audited financial statements remain the authoritative record.
  9. Account termination. Access is revoked when your officer term or CAUFA membership ends.
  10. Changes to these terms. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Terms.
  11. Concerns or complaints. Raise data privacy concerns with the Secretary first, or the National Privacy Commission if unresolved.
