Secured by ISUCauFA System Protection

How this portal protects your account and data.

Sign-in & Zero-Trust

  • Mandatory email verification code on every sign-in — no bypass, no "trusted device" skip.
  • One active session per account; new login revokes the old one instantly.
  • Sessions expire after 8 hours with automatic logout on inactivity.
  • This browser is cryptographically bound to your session; a stolen session cookie alone cannot be replayed elsewhere.
  • Too many wrong passwords temporarily locks sign-in; suspicious activity triggers step-up verification.
  • Screen lock with heartbeat monitoring on officer workstations.

Sessions & Cookies

  • Essential cookies only: sign-in session, forgery-protection token, and device-protection pair. No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Cookies are locked to this site, hidden from scripts where possible, and sent over HTTPS only in production.
  • Device cookies are wiped automatically when you log out.

Encryption

  • Passwords protected with Argon2id; contact details, MFA secrets, and health information stored encrypted.
  • All traffic encrypted with modern TLS (HTTPS); database backups stored encrypted outside the public web folder.
  • Uploaded documents verified by content (not filenames) and checked for duplicates and malicious files.

Network & Transport

  • HTTPS-only in production with automatic HTTP-to-HTTPS redirect — sign-ins never travel over plaintext.
  • Modern TLS 1.2+ connections with HSTS, so browsers refuse insecure downgrades.
  • Emails (including verification codes) sent over encrypted SMTP; the database connection supports TLS encryption.
  • Real-time dashboards accept connections from this site only.

Threat Protection

  • Cross-site request forgery blocked on every form, including automated origin checks.
  • Content-Security-Policy headers restrict what pages may load or submit to.
  • Bot screening on sign-in; every sensitive read and change is written to a tamper-evident audit trail.
