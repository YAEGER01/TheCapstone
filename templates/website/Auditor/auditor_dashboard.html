Auditor Help Guide

Role-specific help for the Auditor dashboard

Your Role: As auditor, you verify recorded entries and monitor compliance before they reach the President for final approval.

This guide walks you through these tasks step by step:

  • Monthly Dues Process: audit and verify the recorded deductions.
  • Verifications & Auditing: verify aid requests and generated reports.
  • Unpaid Members Monitoring: track unpaid dues and send email notifications.
Note: Audit and verify the recorded deductions before final approval.

Follow these steps to audit the monthly dues:

1.
Navigate to Deduction Verification: Go to Audit on the main menu and select Verify Deductions.
2.
View Pending Entries: Locate the pending record on the list, and click the item to open its full details.
3.
Review and Verify Details: Inspect the transaction breakdown, amounts, and member allocation details to ensure all records are accurate.
4.
Forward for Final Approval: After verifying the information is correct, click the button to Submit for Final Approval to send the audited entry on for final sign-off.
Note: Review and verify the Treasurer’s submissions before sending them to the President for final approval.
1.
Aid Requests Verification: Navigation: Audit > Verify Aid Requests
  • View pending Aid Requests.
  • Review the request details.
  • Click Submit to send the entry to the President.
2.
Reports Verification: Navigation: Audit > Reports For Verification
  • View pending generated Reports.
  • Review the report contents.
  • Click Submit to forward the report to the President.
Note: Track unpaid members and notify them by email.

Navigation: Compliance > Deduction Compliance

1.
Open Deduction Compliance to view the list of departments.
2.
Select a department to view its unpaid members for the monthly due.
3.
Check the checkbox next to the members you want to notify (or select multiple members).
4.
Click Submit to send email notifications to the selected members.
